# Getting started

Kairos asks one thing of you: decide *when* the work happens. Everything else, like projects, domains, routines and focus over time, is optional. Start by planning today, and add the rest when you find you want it.

## Plan today

Kairos keeps your plan in your daily notes, under a `## Schedule` heading. It uses the folder and date format from **Settings → Daily notes** (or the Periodic Notes plugin), and creates a day's note the first time you add something to it.

1. **Open the Day view.** Select the **hourglass** icon in the left ribbon, then **Day**. It opens in the right sidebar.
2. **Make a block.** Press on empty time and drag down. A block is a span of your day, like "09:30 - 11:00 Deep work". Select its title to rename it, and select its time to type an exact one.
3. **Add tasks to it.** Point at the block's top-right corner and select **Add task**. Select the task's text to rename it. A task is something to do; it always lives inside a block.
4. **Check one off.** Select its checkbox: open → half-done → done. Press and hold it to cancel the task.

That's the whole idea: time belongs to blocks, work belongs to tasks.

Ctrl/Cmd+click the date at the top of the Day view to see your note. It's plain markdown, and you can edit it by hand:

```markdown
## Schedule
- 09:30 - 11:00 Deep work
	- [x] Outline chapter 2
	- [ ] Email my supervisor
```

## Move things around

Open the hourglass menu and select **Grid**. Each column is a day.

- **Move a task to another day:** press and hold its text for a moment, then drag it.
- **Schedule it:** with the Day view open beside the Grid, drag a task onto empty time in the Day view. Kairos makes a block around it.
- **Tasks with no time yet** go in **Unscheduled**.

## Name what keeps coming up

After a few days, you'll notice the same kinds of work coming back: thesis work, the gym, groceries. That's the time to name them.

1. Right-click a block or task and select **Add association**.
2. Type a name, like "Thesis", and select **Create project "Thesis"**.

The block's line now ends in `[Thesis]`, its tasks belong to Thesis too, and Thesis gets its own row in the Grid.

- A **project** is something you focus on for a while, like a thesis or a hackathon. It isn't finished; it's active or not.
- A **domain** is a broad area of your life, like School or Health. Create one the same way, with **Create domain**. In **Projects & domains** (from the hourglass menu), right-click a project and select **Change domain** to put it in one.

You never have to file anything. A task with no project sits on the Grid's **Unassigned** row, and that's fine.

## When you want more

Each of these is optional. The [Guide](guide.md) covers them all.

- **Routines:** right-click a block or task and select **Repeat…**. Kairos then offers it on the days it's due.
- **Backlog:** things you mean to do but haven't given a day. Give one a date and it comes back to you then.
- **Notes:** a bullet under a block without a checkbox records what happened.
- **Focus over time:** record when a project is active and how hard you're pushing it, and see it in the **Timeline** view.

## Things that are easy to miss

- **Point at a row's top-right corner** to see its actions. A small colored fold marks the spot when you point at the row.
- **Right-click** almost anything for more.
- **Press and hold a task's text, then move,** to drag it. A quick click edits the text.
- **Hold Ctrl/Cmd while dropping** to copy instead of move.
- **Ctrl/Cmd+click** a date to open that day's note.
- **Point at cut-off text** to read all of it.
